READING NOTES · 5 MIN READ

A calmer way to read the web.

A different language should never stand between you and a good idea. Read the pages you care about, in words that feel like home.

Read naturally, keep the context

A good translation does more than replace words. It follows the context, keeps the flow of a sentence, and helps the author's ideas come through naturally.

See the documentation for more details.

Keep the details that matter

Links and formatting stay in place, while technical identifiers remain intact. For example, response.json() stays readable as code.

Read at your own pace. Compare the original with its translation, or select just one sentence when you need a little help.

Where context comes from

Context is the surrounding information that helps determine what a word means here. Nearby paragraphs, definitions elsewhere on the page, and linked sections all provide useful clues.
